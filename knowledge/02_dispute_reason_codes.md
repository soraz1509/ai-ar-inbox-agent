# Dispute Reason Code Catalogue - BestRun DE
<!-- Synthetic demo catalogue. In SAP FSCM Dispute Management, reasons are customer-configured; codes below are a demo mapping. -->

## How to use the catalogue
Choose exactly one reason code per dispute case. If no code fits, use OTHER and escalate to the AR
team lead. The dispute case title states the reason in one line; the note contains the customer's
own words and the policy basis.

## PROMO - Promotion or trade allowance deduction
Customer deducts a promotion allowance, campaign rebate or advertising contribution.
Typical wording: Aktionsrabatt, Werbekostenzuschuss (WKZ), Promotion, campaign rebate, trade allowance.
Owner: Sales key account manager. Evidence required: promo claim number and agreement reference.
Valid only as defined in the customer agreement.

## PRICE - Price difference
Customer pays a different price than invoiced. Typical wording: Preisdifferenz, falscher Preis,
agreed price, price list. Owner: Sales order management. Evidence: order confirmation or contract price.

## QUANTITY - Quantity difference or short delivery
Customer pays for fewer units than invoiced. Typical wording: Mindermenge, Fehlmenge, short delivery,
missing items. Owner: Logistics. Evidence: proof of delivery, delivery note.

## DAMAGED - Damaged or defective goods
Customer deducts for damaged or defective goods. Typical wording: beschädigt, Transportschaden,
defekt, damaged, broken. Owner: Quality / customer service. Evidence: photos, damage report, return number.

## FREIGHT - Freight or delivery cost deduction
Customer deducts freight, packaging or delivery charges. Typical wording: Frachtkosten, Versandkosten,
freight, shipping charges. Owner: Logistics. Evidence: Incoterms and contract.

## DUPLICATE - Duplicate invoice or already paid
Customer claims the invoice was invoiced twice or already paid. Typical wording: doppelt berechnet,
bereits bezahlt, duplicate, already paid. Owner: AR. Evidence: customer payment proof.

## UNEARNED_DISCOUNT - Cash discount taken after discount period
Customer took cash discount (Skonto) after the discount period. Owner: AR collector.
Evidence: payment value date versus discount due date.

## OTHER - Anything else
Use only if no other code fits. Escalate to AR team lead.
