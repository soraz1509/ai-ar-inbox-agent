## 10100013 Inlandskunde Invoice List - promotion allowance agreement
<!-- customer: 10100013 -->
Annual promotion agreement (Aktionsvereinbarung) 2022-2026. The customer may deduct a promotion
allowance (Aktionsrabatt) of up to 2 % of the invoice value for campaigns agreed with Sales.
Each deduction must quote a promo claim number in the format PC-YYYY-NNNN.
Without a valid promo claim number the deduction is not accepted automatically: create a dispute
case with reason PROMO for validation by the Sales key account manager, create the payment advice
for the amount actually paid, and keep the deducted amount open as residual.
Payment terms: 60 days net (NT60). Correspondence in German.

## CASH_APP HYPERCOM AG - remittance via payment advice numbers
<!-- customer: CASH_APP -->
HYPERCOM AG pays through its central treasury and always quotes payment advice numbers (PAYADV...)
that are stored as reference on our invoices. No deduction rights and no cash discount.
Account CASH_APP is separate from account 10100001 Hypercom AG (same legal name, different business
relationship); never mix the two accounts. Correspondence in English.

## 10100001 Hypercom AG - purchase order references
<!-- customer: 10100001 -->
The customer's accounts payable system quotes only its purchase order numbers (format 45000xxxxx)
on payments. We store the customer PO number in the assignment field of the invoice, so payments
can be matched via the PO number. No deduction rights. Payment terms: 30 days net (NT30).
Correspondence in English.
